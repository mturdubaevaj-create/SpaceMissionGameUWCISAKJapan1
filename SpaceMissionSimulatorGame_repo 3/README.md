# Space Mission Simulator
**by UWC ISAK-1 · NASA Space Apps Challenge**

A retro pixel-art game where you design a spacecraft, launch it through Earth's atmosphere, choose a
trajectory, survive deep-space events and collect science at 8 destinations, ending at a black hole.
Then design your own multi-stop missions.

## ▶ Play
- **In your browser:** `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/` (built automatically from this repository)
- **On your computer:** install [Python 3](https://www.python.org/downloads/), download this repository
  (**Code → Download ZIP**), extract it, open a terminal in the `SpaceMissionSimulator` folder and run:
  ```
  pip install pygame-ce
  python main.py
  ```

**Controls:** mouse for menus · arrow keys / WASD and SPACE in mini-games · SPACE/ENTER advances dialogue · F11 fullscreen.

## What you learn
- **Rocket science:** the rocket equation, thrust-to-weight, delta-v, Max-Q, heat shields, escape velocity, Hohmann transfers and gravity assists.
- **Real destinations:** temperatures, gravity, atmospheres and hazards from NASA's Planetary Fact Sheets.
- **Real NASA missions:** every mission report compares your trip with Apollo 11, Perseverance, Magellan, Juno, Cassini, Parker Solar Probe or OSIRIS-REx.
- **Space weather:** solar flare events are based on real X-class flares from NASA's DONKI database (downloaded live when online).
- **33 space facts** in the Fact Book, tested by an alien pirate's quiz, plus 42 science quiz questions.

## Features
Ship building with a WHY? explainer · safe vs. direct trajectories · ship-system alerts · science challenges ·
solar flare shelter, hull breach, asteroid field, wiring and star-tracker repairs, docking · cargo mini-games
(lander, rover, satellite, telescope, lab) · two black hole endings · **Design Mission** (launch from Earth,
the Moon, Mars or Bennu and visit up to 5 destinations with the Interplanetary Station).

## Data & sources
NASA Planetary Fact Sheets (NSSDCA) · NASA DONKI space weather database · NASA mission pages · JPL Small-Body
Database · Event Horizon Telescope · Gaia BH1. Budgets, turn counts and costs are simplified game values.
